fix: PDF attachments render via offline pdf.js

Android WebView (Fully Kiosk) has no native PDF renderer, unlike
desktop Chrome, so PDF attachments in the messages modal failed to
display. Adds a bundled offline pdf.js viewer (vendor/pdfjs/) that
renders pages to canvas instead.

Also routes file-modal by extension generally: images go straight to
the existing lightbox, plain text still uses the iframe as before, and
anything WebView can't render (docx/xlsx/pptx/zip/unknown) now shows a
clear "can't preview, download instead" fallback rather than silently
failing in an empty iframe.
